========================================
  KHAN CLOTH SHOP - PHOTO UPLOAD GUIDE
========================================

Aap ab asani se product photos laga sakte hain!

----------------------------------------
STEP 1: Photos Taiyar Karein
----------------------------------------
- Apni product photos mobile ya computer se lein
- Photos ko rename karein:
    product1.jpg  →  Premium Lawn 3PC
    product2.jpg  →  Khaddar Winter Suit
    product3.jpg  →  Gents Wash & Wear
    product4.jpg  →  Embroidered Fancy Suit
    product5.jpg  →  Linen 3 Piece
    product6.jpg  →  Kids Fancy Dress

- Best size: 800 x 800 pixels (square) ya 800 x 1000
- Format: JPG, PNG, WEBP sab chalega

----------------------------------------
STEP 2: Images Folder Mein Dalen
----------------------------------------
Website folder mein "images" naam ka folder hai.

Uske andar apni 6 photos copy karein:

images/
   ├── product1.jpg
   ├── product2.jpg
   ├── product3.jpg
   ├── product4.jpg
   ├── product5.jpg
   └── product6.jpg

----------------------------------------
STEP 3: Browser Mein Check Karein
----------------------------------------
index.html file ko double-click karke browser mein kholen.
Aapki photos products pe show ho jayengi!

Agar photo nahi dikhe to:
- File name exact same hona chahiye (product1.jpg etc.)
- images folder sahi jagah pe hona chahiye

----------------------------------------
EXTRA: Photo Change Karna
----------------------------------------
Jab bhi naya photo lagana ho:
1. Nayi photo ko same name se save karein (jaise product1.jpg)
2. Purani photo ki jagah replace kar dein
3. Browser refresh karein

----------------------------------------
Note:
----------------------------------------
Agar aapko zyada products add karne hain to mujhe bata dein,
main website mein aur products + photos ka option add kar doonga.
